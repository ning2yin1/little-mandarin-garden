# 生成画像のプロンプト

制作方式：内蔵画像生成ツール。キャラクター画像は透過指定。生成後の処理は素材用アトラスの分割・余白調整・書き出し。画像の絵柄自体は再描画していません。
SVGのボタン・アイコンはUI用に別途作成。下記は画像生成に送ったプロンプトです。

## 1. クマ6ポーズ（transparent_background=true）
Use case: illustration-story. Production game asset for Little Mandarin Garden, a gentle watercolor and colored-pencil picture book Chinese study app. Make ONE transparent PNG character atlas, 3 columns by 2 rows, evenly spaced same square cells, large clear transparent gutters. Six separate full-body poses of the SAME ORIGINAL tiny apricot teddy bear with rounded head, small close-set black eyes, ivory muzzle, sage green gingham scarf and small crimson heart fabric patch on belly. Original character, not any existing franchise character. Poses reading left to right: waving welcome, holding open book, joyfully celebrating with paws raised, thoughtful with paw on cheek, gently encouraging holding little heart, sleeping curled beside book. Soft fluffy plush texture, fine warm brown pencil outlines, handpainted pale apricot and cream, vintage nursery scrapbook aesthetic matching reference mood. No lettering, no frames, no backgrounds, no floor shadows extending into other cells. Each character entirely inside its own cell, consistent scale. True alpha transparency.

## 2. 庭の紙背景（transparent_background=false）
Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: portrait mobile study app background for Little Mandarin Garden. A delicate vintage watercolor and fine colored pencil garden frame on warm ivory paper, portrait 1024 by 1536 composition. Large quiet warm ivory EMPTY central area occupying 78% of image for readable app UI. Top 15 percent: pale powder-blue sky wash, airy little clouds, a branch of tiny sage green leaves entering upper left. Bottom 15 percent: handpainted meadow border of tiny daisies, red little hearts, yellow buttercups, clover and pale grass. Very light sparse flowers on extreme side edges only. No characters, no lettering, no UI, no buttons, no panels, no logo, no watermark. Fine nostalgic storybook texture, warm cream #fff9eb, sage #91aa82, butter #f1d58a, accents cranberry #d95874. Art should frame and never compete with study text. Refined hand drawn small details, no plastic 3D, no gradients of saturated pink. Full bleed portrait.

## 3. ウサギと小鳥（transparent_background=true）
Use case: illustration-story. A transparent PNG production character atlas for Little Mandarin Garden, TWO separate original plush animal friends in two equal square cells side by side, huge transparent gutter between them. Left cell a pale sage-green floppy-eared plush bunny wearing a tiny butter-yellow linen apron with a crimson heart pocket, holding a daisy, seated 3/4 view. Right cell a pale lavender plump plush sparrow with cream cheeks, tiny brown beak, sage gingham bow, hugging a little closed red book. Both have original friendly simple black dot eyes, warm pencil outlines and delicate watercolor plush fur. Match a vintage picturebook apricot teddy bear with sage gingham scarf and heart patch. Not existing franchise characters. Entire full bodies well inside each cell. Soft matte watercolor and fine colored pencil, cream pale pastel colors. No text no labels no background no ground plane no cast shadows no frames. Actual alpha transparent background.
